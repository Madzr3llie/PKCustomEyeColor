# PKCustomEyeColor
Allows HSV sliders to control eye color. Passes in nesting. Comes with camera controls for easier editing.

## Pan & Zoom
For your convenience on some of the smallest to largest creatures, a pan and zoom feature has been added to the animal nursery preview.   
Do not worry, rotate is still there. 

<ins>Controls:</ins>\
Pan = Right Mouse + Drag\
Rotate = Left Mouse + Drag\
Zoom = Left Shift + Scroll Wheel

## Breeding
The custom eye colors have been made to fully pass on in reproduction with some additional stats below.

<ins>80%</ins> chance for offspring to inherit one or the other parent's eye color.\
<ins>20%</ins> chance for the eye color to be a mix of both parents.\
Youngling eye colors tend to be much darker than their parents. Give them time to settle into their adult colors. You can see this transition yourself using the maturity slider in the animal nursery preview.

## Warning & Tips
This mod does not come without some strange behaviors, of which I cannot fully patch out due to Unity limitations and as a consequence for how the mod was able to be accomplished. 
The creature default eye is red upon loading into the animal nursery preview, but upon randomizing the skin - it will go black.
Because eyes go black upon skin randomizing, it is advised you edit the skin first to your liking before messing with eyes. Eyes have their own randomize button that does not touch the skin! 

Some species have different shader information for the eyes, and they may still appear very dark even with max value. 

Keep in mind, if you randomize mutations and make no move to edit their eye color, it will pull from whatever values the HSV sliders are on for breeding inheritance information, regardless of the color of their eye through mutation. You need to make an edit to it if you wish for specific colors to be in the "gene pool." My suggestion is to edit the skin and eye to your liking, then turn on the mutation.
Speaking of mutations, if you edit the eye AFTER turning on the mutation, it will snap to whatever HSV value was there before. Keep this in mind if you wish to keep the randomized mutation eye color. 
Note: Mutation being albino, leucistic, & melanistic.
